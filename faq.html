Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding clinic hours, insurance, and lab reports

Outpatient clinics are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Our Emergency Room, Laboratory, and Pharmacy operate 24/7.

We accept all major national and international medical insurance plans. Please bring your valid insurance card and photo ID upon arrival.

General ward visits are open daily from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM. ICU visiting windows are scheduled to protect patient recovery.

Secure digital links are sent directly to your registered email and phone once verified, or printed at our Medical Records Desk.
